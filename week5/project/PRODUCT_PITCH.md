# UltraMedia Race Desk

**Turn race updates into evidence-backed stories.**

Race updates arrive fast. Every story needs facts. UltraMedia brings race evidence, draft stories and editorial review into one workspace.

![UltraMedia product pitch](../diagrams/ultramedia-product-pitch.png)

## Who it is for

- Race organizers preparing event updates.
- Sports editors reviewing source-backed reporting.
- Event media teams developing short stories and social captions.
- Running communities sharing race coverage.

These are intended audiences, not claims of existing customers or deployments.

## How it works

1. Choose a race moment: position changes, pace shifts, cutoff updates or record projections.
2. Bring the evidence together: timing facts and course context support the selected moment.
3. Prepare a cited draft: generate structured story text with source references.
4. Check before sharing: inspect output format, citation IDs and numeric claims; flag missing or conflicting evidence.
5. Keep the editor in control: inspect sources, correct wording and approve a version.

Automated checks assist editorial judgment. They do not prove every sentence is correct, and approval does not automatically publish externally.
